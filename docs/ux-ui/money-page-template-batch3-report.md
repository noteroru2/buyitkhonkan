## Scope

- 15 category money pages
- 6 brand pages
- 21 model/series pages
- 5 condition pages
- 8 released local pages

Architecture skeleton pages remain on their existing `HOLD_NOINDEX` path and do not receive the released-money-page branch.

## UX/UI changes

### 1. Two-column conversion hero

- Existing page H1 is preserved.
- Main SEO intro remains in the hero.
- Added service trust signals.
- Added a dedicated LINE conversion card alongside the hero content on desktop.
- Responsive layout collapses to one column on tablet/mobile.

### 2. LINE assessment card

New reusable component: `src/components/MoneyConversionCard.astro`

The card tells sellers exactly what to send:

1. Product photos
2. Model/specification
3. Actual condition / defects
4. Area in Khon Kaen

LINE is the primary action; phone remains secondary elsewhere in the template.

### 3. Quick section navigation

New reusable component: `src/components/MoneyPageNav.astro`

Category money pages expose direct anchors for:

- Accepted models / conditions
- Price factors
- Selling process
- Service area

Brand/model/condition/local detail pages expose direct anchors for important information, related pages when available, and the assessment process.

### 4. Mid-content LINE CTA

A compact conversion panel was inserted at a natural decision point in both category and detail templates. This reduces the need to scroll back to the hero when the visitor is ready to submit a device.

### 5. Final CTA for detail pages

Brand/model/condition/local detail pages now end with a full-width LINE-first conversion section, matching the category-money-page conversion pattern.

### 6. Conversion tracking context

New CTAs include `data-cta-location` values such as:

- `money_hero`
- `money_hero_card`
- `money_mid_content`
- `detail_hero`
- `detail_hero_card`
- `detail_mid_content`
- `detail_final`

The existing Base layout tracking handler remains unchanged and will carry these CTA locations into `dataLayer`.
